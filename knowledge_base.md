# Pixel Studio: customer support knowledge base

Pixel Studio is a small web design studio (fictional company used for testing).

## Services and prices
- Landing page (1 page): from $400, ready in 5 working days.
- Business website (up to 7 pages): from $1,200, ready in 3 weeks.
- Online shop (Shopify): from $2,500, ready in 5-6 weeks.
- Website redesign: price after an audit; the audit itself costs $150.
- Monthly support: $90 per month, includes up to 3 hours of small edits.

## Payment
- 50% upfront, 50% after the client approves the finished site.
- We accept bank transfer and card payment. We do not accept cryptocurrency.

## Process
1. Free 20-minute brief by chat or email (no calls needed).
2. Design mockup in Figma, up to 2 rounds of revisions included.
3. Development, then the client tests the site on a private link.
4. Launch and a 30-day free bug-fix period.

## Policies
- Extra revision rounds cost $50 each.
- Hosting and domain are paid by the client; we help set them up for free.
- We do not write blog articles or run ads; we can recommend partners.
- Working hours: Monday-Friday, 10:00-18:00 (Kyiv time). Replies within 1 working day.
- Refunds: the 50% upfront payment is refundable only before the design stage starts.
